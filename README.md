# MCAA Research Group

## Modelling, Control, Automation and Analysis Research Group

Welcome to the official repository of the **MCAA Research Group**.

The group focuses on advanced research in **modelling, control systems, automation, robotics, intelligent systems, and data-driven analysis**. Our work aims to bridge theoretical research with practical engineering applications and develop intelligent, reliable, and autonomous systems for real-world challenges.

## Research Areas

- Modelling and System Analysis
- Control Theory and Control Systems
- Automation and Intelligent Systems
- Robotics and Autonomous Systems
- Machine Learning and Data-Driven Methods
- Human–Robot Collaboration
- Safety-Critical Autonomous Systems
