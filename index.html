Welcome to MCAA Lab

Where theory meets the physical world

MCAA Lab is an interdisciplinary laboratory dedicated to fundamental research and practical deployment of modeling, control, and automation systems. We develop mathematical models of complex phenomena, design controllers that keep systems safe and responsive, and build autonomous machines that operate alongside people.

Our work spans robotic manipulation, autonomous vehicles, digital twins, and data-driven decision support. We collaborate with academic partners, industry, and public agencies to move results from the whiteboard to the field.
