Join the research community

Membership at MCAA is free for the first year and open to students, researchers, and industry partners.

Membership

Why become an MCAA member?

Members become part of a community that shares results, hardware, datasets, and training before they reach the broader public.

  • Early access to publications, datasets, and software.
  • Invitations to seminars, workshops, and the annual symposium.
  • Networking with researchers, alumni, and industry partners.
  • Mentorship and research training for student members.
  • Opportunities to propose collaborative projects.
